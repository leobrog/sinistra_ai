#### The Shared Articles of Communism Interstellar

1) All comrades of Communism Interstellar are equal.

2) All Communist factions and Associates are equal.

3) Each party associated with Communism Interstellar is autonomous and takes full responsibility for its own internal governance and management of its in-game faction.

4) Communism Interstellar adopts all existing treaties and agreements, signed individually by Associates, in both word and spirit/

5) Comrades are free to choose their own course of action, however, any actions undertaken outside those defined within these Articles, cannot be undertaken in the name of Communism Interstellar.

Comrades wishing to take unilateral actions incompatible with these Articles, are requested to drop their squadron tags whilst doing so. Comrades may still be held to account for such actions if they cause reputational, diplomatic or operational harm to Communism Interstellar.

6) EXTERNAL RELATIONS

We will treat other groups with respect. In the event of a disagreement between our groups, we will first of all seek to resolve these differences by diplomatic means, using independent arbitration if required. We will not intentionally carry out hostile actions against other active player groups unless a state of war has been formally declared.

7) RIGHTS OF CONTROL

Communism Interstellar asserts the right of first control over any pre-Trailblazers system where one of our named factions is currently in control, or was deposed by another player group without our agreement. Similarly, we will respect the rights of first control for any active player group* where it can be shown that they had a prior claim. These principles may be modified by formal alliances, agreements, or bilateral treaties.

* Communism Interstellar makes no distinction between player-inserted minor factions (PMFs), and procedurally generated minor factions where they are adopted and/or being actively supported by an organised player group.

8) SYSTEM COLONISATION

Communism Interstellar respects the authorship of System Architects.

Communism Interstellar makes no claim of ownership on the colonies of non-members which incidentally have an associated faction present.

Comrade Architects are free to manage their colonies as they please. Communism Interstellar will defend their claim of first control against hostile players / factions.

Communism Interstellar reserves the right to defend its interests in the sphere of colonisation. This may include taking direct action where necessary.

9) POWERPLAY 2.0

Communism Interstellar has no organisational allegiance for the purposes of Power Play 2.0. Comrades are free to pledge to any power they wish, and are encouraged to develop and maintain positive relations with the Powers present in their maintained territories.

Comrades are encouraged, so far as practicable, to ensure their Powerplay actions are not contrary to the interests of our Comrades or their supported minor factions.

10) RELATIONS WITH HOSTILE NON-HUMAN SPECIES

Communism Interstellar is committed to the defense of communist peoples from all threats, whether anthropogenic or xenogenic. Comrades are permitted to become members of other player groups in an individual capacity for the purposes of anti-xeno defence.

11) UPHOLDING OUR VALUES

Comrades who engage in any form of bigotry or abuse; cheating / exploiting; who are shown to be compromising the security and integrity of Communism Interstellar; or who persistently fail to uphold these Articles; may be summarily suspended by the Cadres for no more than 14 days while an investigation takes place. We believe in helping each-other to be better and will always seek amicable resolutions where possible.

Where a violation or pattern of behaviour is extremely serious, the Cadres may call a vote on the removal of Comrade status from those responsible. Such votes will be open to all Comrades, may last no more than 14 days, and will be decided by simple majority. In the event of a tie, the Cadres will be invited to deliberate, in private, the collective making of a single casting vote.

12) ANTI-BOTTING AGREEMENT

Communism Interstellar is a proud signatory of the Anti-Botting Agreement code of practice. Any comrade caught using automated accounts will be immediately expelled and reported to Frontier Developments.

13) PROCEDURES FOR ALTERING THESE ARTICLES

Any changes to these Shared Articles must be put to a vote open to all Comrades. Voting will open no less than 14 days after any proposal, to allow fair opportunity for discussion and dissent. Changes shall be adopted only if at least ⅔ of votes cast are in favour.

Provision is made separately for the adoption of minor factions into the list above by calling a vote of all comrades, lasting 14 days and decided by simple majority.


#### Associate-Specific Articles


People’s Party of Heverty

The People’s Party of Heverty claims sovereignty over all start systems controlled by the three communist factions of Heverty:

People’s Party of Heverty
Theta Chamaelontis Citizen Party
Left Party of Guarpulici.

Membership within PPH is granted to any Comrade that can demonstrate that they have achieved allied status with the People’s Party of Heverty minor faction.
PPH Members have the ability to influence decisions and direction with regard to the Heverty system and systems under control of PPH, TCCP and LPG.
Any Comrade is welcome to participate in PPH’s discord server.


#### Defined Terms


Comrade: Any Commander who can demonstrate allied status with any Communist faction; wishes to be associated with Communism Interstellar; and agrees to abide by these Articles.

Veteran Comrades: Comrade Commanders who have been cleared for access to sensitive information about our operations. Veterans play a key role in monitoring, maintenance and strategic coordination of the various communist Minor Factions named above.

Due to the need for operational security, Veteran status is granted by invitation only. Any Veteran may nominate a Commander for clearance by posting in the secured channel. The granting of Veteran clearance will be agreed on a case-by-case basis by consensus of the existing Veteran cohort and subject to vetting. Nominations and all deliberations about such matters are to be kept confidential within the secure channel.

Cadres: Cadres form the officer corps of Communism Interstellar, and are responsible for administrative and organisational duties such as managing the in-game squadron and moderation of the Discord server.

Associate: Any Communist organisation that agrees collectively to abide by the shared Articles of Association listed below and wishes to Associate with Communism Interstellar.

Partner: Any squadron, faction or group who formally recognise the rights of Communism Interstellar factions in a reciprocal arrangement.



#### Communism Interstellar Representation Guidelines


If you intend to represent Communism Interstellar on other Discords or, for instance, the Frontier forums, either by referring to your own membership as a part of your post, or by having links/images in your signature that can be connected to the group, then here are some guidelines for you to follow.

1. Do not intentionally antagonise Frontier Developments

- This is poor form and almost certainly a disservice towards the public image of Communism Interstellar. Privately and on our internal Discord you may hold whatever beliefs you wish, but challenging Frontier openly is not appropriate.

2. Do not threaten other people or player groups

- If and when there is cause to go to war with another player group, we will discuss it together on our internal Discord. You should not sidestep this process or complicate diplomatic relations by sending threats to other people. Threatening Frontier Developments is also inappropriate and liable to get you banned from their forums.

3. Do not share information about our operations

- Operational security is of considerable importance. You should not reveal, directly or indirectly, any information which would compromise our activities.
